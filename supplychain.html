4 · Three inflections to watch

Each is a content-migration event with clear winners and a specific signal — the things that actually move estimates.

Copper → Optical (CPO)

NVIDIA's Spectrum-X co-packaged-optics switch is now in mass production — but scale-up (NVLink) stays copper. Jensen: needs MORE copper AND more CPO. Both grow.
Winners
Coherent, Lumentum, Fabrinet, Marvell, Broadcom; copper still: Amphenol, Credo, BizLink
Signal to watch
CPO attach rate on Rubin/Kyber switches; Taiwan optics monthly revenue; EML laser lead times

Air → Liquid → Immersion

Liquid is mandatory from GB200; Rubin Ultra packages hit ~3,600W, pushing toward immersion by 2027. Cooling content rises every generation.
Winners
Boyd (priv.), CoolIT (priv.), Vertiv, AVC, Auras; quick-disconnects: CPC/Colder, Staubli
Signal to watch
Taiwan thermal monthly revenue (AVC, Auras, Kaori); immersion qualification news; CDU capacity

48V → 800V HVDC

Racks go ~130kW → ~1MW (Kyber, 2027). 800V transmits ~150% more power per copper and eliminates 200kg busbars — shifting dollars to GaN/SiC.
Winners
Navitas, Infineon, onsemi, Power Integrations, STMicro, ROHM, TI; systems: Vertiv, Eaton, Delta
Signal to watch
800V design wins on Kyber sidecars; Navitas/Infineon AI-power commentary; Vertiv 800V launch
